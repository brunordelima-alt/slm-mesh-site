SLM Mesh

A personal AI assistant that runs on your own computer.

SLM Mesh answers questions, researches on the web, writes and runs code and carries out tasks with several steps, using small language models that run on the owner's own machine (today, a Mac mini). Conversations and files stay on that machine. It is a personal, self-hosted project: each installation belongs to and is run by its owner, and no data is sent to the developer.

Google Docs

When you ask SLM Mesh for a document as a Google Doc, it uploads the document it wrote to your Google Drive and gives you the link. It asks for your approval before each upload. It requests only the Google Drive drive.file permission, which lets it see and change only the files it creates itself, nothing else in your Drive. You connect your Google account on your own computer (./slm google setup) and can disconnect at any time (./slm google disconnect, or from your Google Account's third-party access page).

Read the privacy policy for what SLM Mesh does with your Google data.
