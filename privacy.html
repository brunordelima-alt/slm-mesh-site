Privacy policy

Last updated 23 November 2026

SLM Mesh is a personal AI assistant that runs on its owner's own computer. This policy explains what data it handles, and in particular what it does with data from your Google account when you connect it.

Who runs SLM Mesh

Each installation of SLM Mesh runs on, and is controlled by, the person who installed it. The developer does not operate a server for it and does not receive your conversations, files or Google data.

Google user data SLM Mesh accesses

SLM Mesh requests a single Google permission: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file. With it, SLM Mesh can create files in your Google Drive and see or change only the files it created. It cannot see, read or change any other file in your Drive, your email, your contacts or anything else in your Google account.

How SLM Mesh uses it

  • When you ask for a document as a Google Doc, SLM Mesh uploads the document it wrote for you to your Google Drive, as a Google Doc, and gives you its link. It asks for your approval before each upload.
  • It never makes a Google Doc from a request that read your private files or your computer's output.
  • It does not read, analyse, sell, share or transfer your Google data, and does not use it for advertising or to train AI models.

Where your sign-in is kept

When you connect your Google account, the sign-in Google returns (an OAuth refresh token) is stored only on your own computer, in SLM Mesh's settings file, readable only by your user account. It is sent only to Google, to create the documents you ask for.

Sharing

SLM Mesh does not share your Google user data with anyone. Documents it creates stay in your Google Drive, under your control, and are shared only if you share them.

Google API Services User Data Policy

SLM Mesh's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and data

  • Run ./slm google disconnect on your computer: it revokes SLM Mesh's access and deletes the stored sign-in.
  • Or remove SLM Mesh at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Documents SLM Mesh created stay in your Drive until you delete them.

Other data

Your conversations and files stay on your computer. Web searches and page fetches you ask for go through a web search provider, and messages sent through Telegram pass through Telegram's servers, under their own policies.

Changes and contact
